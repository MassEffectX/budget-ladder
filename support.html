Common Questions

Where is my data stored? Budget entries and attachments are stored locally on your device in the current build.

Why does the app ask for camera, microphone, speech recognition, or photo access? These permissions support receipt photos, expense videos, imported media, and voice transcripts. They are used only after you choose one of those capture actions.

Can I edit an expense? Yes. Open the expense feed and tap a ledger entry or its pencil button to update it.

Does Budget Ladder require an account? No. The current build does not require account creation.
