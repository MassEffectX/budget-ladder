Privacy Policy

Local-first by design.

Last updated: April 24, 2026

Budget Ladder is designed as a local-first budgeting app. In the current build, budget entries, expense notes, receipt photos, short videos, and dictated transcripts stay on your device unless you choose to share media or save a copy back to Photos.

Data Collection

Budget Ladder does not collect, sell, or share personal data in the current local-only build. The app does not require an account and does not upload your expense records to a server.

Device Permissions

Camera access is used only when you choose to capture receipt photos or short expense videos. Microphone and speech recognition access are used only when you choose to dictate expense details. Photo library access is used only when you import media or save a copy back to Photos.

Local Storage

Expense records and app data are stored locally on your device. Removing the app may remove locally stored app data depending on your device settings and backup configuration.
