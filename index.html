Fast capture

Expenses can start messy.

Type the amount, attach a receipt, import media, record a short video, or dictate notes. Drafts can be completed later when life slows down.

Beyond the month

Run the long view next to the daily one.

The retirement calculator compares daily, weekly, bi-weekly, and monthly contribution schedules so long-term decisions can sit beside everyday spending.

Privacy posture

Designed to stay on your device.

Budget entries, receipts, videos, and dictated transcripts are stored locally. Budget Ladder does not require its own account. Optional online AI starts off; after you approve a provider and explicitly request a fill, only the selected expense text and date context are sent to that provider.
